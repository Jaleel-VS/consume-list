# Spanish-language audio podcasts and dramas

Spanish-language fiction, horror stories, mysteries, and thrillers. Entries are alphabetical, not ranked. `Queued` means saved for a future listen; recommendations are not personal ratings.

## Known favourite

*Historias de Medianoche* is my current favourite in Spanish. The exact feed has not been confirmed. Research used the likely match, [Chicho Ibáñez Serrador's Podium series](https://www.podiumpodcast.com/podcasts/historias-de-medianoche-podium-os/), which combines narration with acted horror and suspense stories. That attribution remains provisional because other shows share the name.

The selections below include both literary storytelling and serial dramas. They cover different genres rather than requiring every show to combine horror, mystery, and suspense.

## Where to start

### Closest to the horror-story reference

*Noviembre Nocturno* is the first recommendation for narrated literary horror. Its catalogue includes Poe, Lovecraft, and other writers of the fantastic. Expect introductions and literary discussion as well as the story itself, rather than uninterrupted dramatic action. The creator's site also links to its YouTube channel and iVoox archive.

*Cuentos de la casa de la bruja* is another story-first option. It offers mystery as well as horror, making the Christie selection useful for the whodunit interest. Its platform listings mix stories, supporter exclusives, and role-playing recordings; the entire feed is not one serial narrative.

*Ficción sonora* is the alternative for actors interacting in a standalone production. The selected *Extraños en un tren* is a psychological crime thriller about a sinister pact, not a puzzle about an unidentified killer. It offers a different kind of suspense from *Deadly Manners*.

### Serial dramas to broaden the list

1. *Caso 63* is the first serial to try. Its opening uses conversations between two central characters, making it a different experience from a large ensemble drama. It is the Spanish original behind [*Case 63* in the English catalogue](audio-podcasts-dramas.md#podcasts), so listening to that adaptation first would reveal shared story material. Spotify announced the fifth and final season in October 2025.
2. *La firma de Dios* is the compact choice for scientific and philosophical mystery. It won the 2023 Ondas Globales awards for best fiction podcast and best script. The pandemic premise matters, but the questions it raises extend beyond survival. Start with the drama, not its discussion companion.
3. *Blum* is the choice for following clues through an investigation. It resembles a nonfiction podcast but is scripted fiction. The Swiss tourism sponsorship is part of its production history; expect travel and art to occupy more space than in a horror serial. The linked feed is Spanish; an English edition also exists.
4. *El gran apagón* is the closest option here to the societal-collapse side of *We're Alive*. The threat is a blackout rather than zombies. Its multiple viewpoints make it a larger commitment than *Blum* or *La firma de Dios*.
5. *Guerra 3* expands the list into espionage and political suspense. It is a longer geopolitical story, not supernatural horror or a closed-circle whodunit.

## Sources and access notes

Researched on November 9, 2026. Show pages establish the premises and formats. Reviews and listener discussions inform the recommendations but do not make the ranking objective. No personal ratings or completion history have been inferred.

- [Historias de Medianoche, Podium](https://www.podiumpodcast.com/podcasts/historias-de-medianoche-podium-os/): The likely reference edition, with Chicho's narration and actor credits.
- [Noviembre Nocturno's official site](https://noviembrenocturno.es/): Story catalogue, audio links, and production context.
- [iVoox's narrated-fiction roundup](https://www.ivoox.com/blog/los-mejores-podcasts-de-relatos-y-audiolibros/): Platform recommendations, including Noviembre Nocturno and Cuentos de la casa de la bruja. Promotional context, not independent criticism.
- [Caso 63 finale announcement, Spotify](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-10-14/caso-63-podcast-original-de-spotify-temporada-5/): Original cast and the five-season conclusion. Contains later-season plot details.
- [Caso 63 listener discussion, r/Spanish](https://www.reddit.com/r/Spanish/comments/kje3pt/you_must_listen_to_this_podcast_series_caso_63/): Reactions to the opening season, not evidence of current availability.
- [Blum, creator Manuel Bartual](https://manuelbartual.com/blum): Nine-episode structure, Spanish and English versions, free listening links, and Swiss tourism sponsorship.
- [La firma de Dios launch, PRISA](https://www.prisa.com/podium-podcast-estrena-la-firma-de-dios/): Cast, eight story episodes, and the separate discussion companion. Its [current Podium page](https://www.podiumpodcast.com/podcasts/la-firma-de-dios-podium-os/) lists both Ondas awards.
- [Spanish fiction recommendations, El Confidencial](https://www.elconfidencial.com/comunicacion/2023-01-20/mejores-podcast-ficcion-espanol_3559804/): Critical recommendations for El gran apagón, Guerra 3, and La firma de Dios. Some other entries are discussion shows and were not selected here.

Podium provides public listening links for its selected dramas; RNE provides a listening page for the selected adaptation. Some Cuentos episodes require a subscription. Direct iVoox episode pages returned HTTP 451 to the research client, so playback there has not been verified. Listings and links were checked, not playback in every app or country.

*Terror y Nada Más* is also worth investigating for dramatized horror, but access was too uncertain to put it in the main queue. Its [Apple listing](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/terror-y-nada-m%C3%A1s-ficci%C3%B3n-sonora-audiolibros/id1706983002) displayed zero episodes, its website failed to load, and the [Podimo archive](https://podimo.com/es/shows/terror-y-nada-mas) includes an old disabled-feed notice. Those observations do not establish that every archive is unavailable.
